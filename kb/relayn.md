## What RelayN is

RelayN is a unified, AI-powered customer communication platform for businesses.
It brings WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook conversations into a single shared
inbox so a team can manage every customer interaction from one place.

## Unified inbox

- One inbox for WhatsApp, Instagram DM, and Facebook Messenger.
- Conversations from every channel appear in the same thread view.
- Team members can be assigned to conversations and collaborate with internal
  notes.

## AI-powered support and automation

- AI-assisted replies draft responses for agents to review and send.
- Automated workflows handle routine questions and route conversations.
- Broadcast messaging sends updates to many customers at once.
- Lead-management tools track and score conversations by buying intent.

## Business communication management

- Product catalogs let customers browse and enquire inside chat.
- Team collaboration: roles, assignment, internal notes.
- Analytics and reporting on response times, volumes, and outcomes.
- Integrations connect RelayN to other business tools.

# What RelayN AI Does

RelayN is positioned as an **AI-powered customer messaging platform for businesses**.

## Main Idea
- Bring WhatsApp + Instagram + Facebook conversations into one dashboard and add AI/automation on top.
- Instead of a business employee switching between:
  - WhatsApp
  - Instagram
  - Facebook Messenger
- RelayN provides **one unified inbox**.
- The website describes it as an “**all-in-one AI messaging hub**.”

## Features
1. **Unified Inbox**
   - Messages from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook are brought into one interface.
   - The goal is that the business doesn't need to manage each platform separately.
2. **AI Assistant**
   - Helps generate responses and learns from conversations to assist teams in responding faster.
   - Likely the part currently being developed from an engineering perspective.
3. **Real-time Synchronization**
   - Messages are synchronized across connected platforms in real time.
4. **Smart Broadcasting**
   - Send targeted messages using segmentation and scheduling instead of manual individual messaging.
5. **Analytics**
   - Provides insights on response times, engagement, team performance.
6. **Product Catalog**
   - Share products directly through conversations with previews, pricing, checkout links.

## How a Business Joins
The onboarding process involves three steps:
1. Connect accounts
2. Customize setup
3. Start messaging

### Step 1 — Connect Accounts
- Connect WhatsApp Business, Instagram, Facebook (using OAuth; credentials aren't stored).
### Step 2 — Configure the System
- Set AI tone, team roles, automated workflows via dashboard.
### Step 3 — Start Messaging
- After connection, the unified inbox becomes available.
- The homepage advertises “Start Free Today” with no credit card required, a 14-day free trial, and cancel anytime.

## Common questions

**Q: Which channels does RelayN support?**
A: WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger, managed from one shared inbox.

**Q: Does RelayN use AI to answer customers?**
A: Yes — it offers AI-assisted replies and automated workflows. A human can
review AI drafts before they are sent.

**Q: Can I message many customers at once?**
A: Yes, through broadcast messaging.

**Q: Can multiple team members work the same inbox?**
A: Yes — conversations can be assigned, and teammates can leave internal notes.
